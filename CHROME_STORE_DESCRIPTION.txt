================================================================================
WEBSITE HIGHLIGHT SAVER — CHROME WEB STORE DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

Website Highlight Saver is your ultimate AI-powered reading companion and knowledge capture tool for Google Chrome. Seamlessly highlight, organize, and summarize text on any webpage with zero workflow interruption.

Whether you are a student conducting research, a professional analyzing reports, a writer collecting insights, or a lifelong learner reading articles, Website Highlight Saver transforms how you capture and digest information online.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

★ IN-PAGE SMART TOOLTIP & AI SUMMARY
• Highlight text on any webpage to trigger an instant interactive tooltip.
• Save highlights with a single click or tap "AI Summary" for immediate real-time explanations.
• Smart AI Mode:
  - 1–2 Words: Performs a rich word lookup (definitions, part of speech, etymology, example sentences, synonyms, and usage tips).
  - Short Phrases: Provides context, real-world usage examples, and related terms.
  - Paragraphs & Long Text: Delivers key takeaways, bullet points, and core summaries instantly in an in-page modal dialog.

★ CLOUD & LOCAL SYNCHRONIZATION
• Cloud Backed: Automatically syncs your highlights to a secure, serverless database (powered by Convex).
• Cross-Device Persistence: Access your saved highlights across browsers and sessions.
• Offline Fallback: Local storage keeps your data available even when offline.

★ FEATURE-RICH POPUP DASHBOARD
• Searchable Highlight Vault: Instantly filter through saved highlights by title, URL, or content using real-time search.
• Full Date & Time Tracking: Every highlight records localized timestamps so you know exactly when and where you found key insights.
• 10-Item Paginated View: Clean, scrollable list with fast pagination controls (Prev/Next) for seamless navigation.
• Quick Actions: Expand long text cards, jump to original source URLs, or delete unwanted entries with one click.
• AI Digest: Generate a combined AI summary across all your saved highlights in one click.

★ PRIVACY & ISOLATION BY DESIGN
• Shadow DOM Insulated: All in-page tooltips and AI dialogs run inside a Shadow DOM. Host webpage CSS cannot mess up your interface.
• Secure Authentication: Private user accounts via encrypted password sessions.
• Your Data Belongs to You: Export, copy, or manage your stored reading lists whenever you need them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. INSTALL & SIGN IN
   Click "Add to Chrome" and open the extension popup from your toolbar to sign in or create an account.

2. HIGHLIGHT & CAPTURE
   Select any text on Wikipedia, blogs, news sites, or documentation. A floating tooltip will appear above your selection.

3. INSIGHTS AT A GLANCE
   Click "Save Highlight" to add it to your dashboard, or click "AI Summary" to read an instant AI breakdown right inside your current tab.

4. REVIEW YOUR DASHBOARD
   Click the extension icon anytime to browse, search, paginate, or summarize your collected highlights in one place.
