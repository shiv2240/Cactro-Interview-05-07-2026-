================================================================================
WEBSITE HIGHLIGHT SAVER — CHROME WEB STORE DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

Website Highlight Saver is your AI-powered reading companion for Google Chrome. Highlight text, capture insights, and get instant AI explanations on any webpage — without leaving the tab.

Whether you are a student researching, a professional reviewing reports, a writer collecting ideas, or a lifelong learner browsing articles, Website Highlight Saver helps you capture and understand what matters online.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

★ IN-PAGE SELECTION TOOLTIP
• Select text on any webpage to open a floating tooltip.
• Save — store the highlight in your dashboard (synced to the cloud).
• AI Summary — get an instant explanation of the selected text in an in-page modal.
• Summarize Page — generate a structured page summary (Overview, Agenda, Key Takeaways).

★ KEYWORD INSIGHTS (AUTO HIGHLIGHTS)
• A movable Keywords tile appears on the page and analyzes important terms automatically.
• Key words are highlighted in soft sticky-note colors so they stand out without clutter.
• Click the tile to see all detected words; click a word for a sectioned AI summary:
  Meaning · On this page · Why it matters · Quick tip
• From the explanation popup: Copy to clipboard or Save to your dashboard for later.
• Press Esc to close explanation popups. Drag the tile anywhere — it remembers its place.

★ SMART AI MODES (SELECTION SUMMARY)
• Short selections: word/phrase explanations with clear, useful context.
• Longer selections: concise key takeaways and structured insights.
• Page summaries: Overview, main topics, and actionable takeaways.

★ CLOUD & LOCAL SYNC
• Highlights sync to a secure Convex backend when you are signed in.
• Local storage keeps your data available as a reliable fallback.
• Access your reading list across sessions from the extension popup.

★ POPUP DASHBOARD
• Browse saved highlights with domain favicons and pagination.
• Search, expand, open the source URL, or delete entries quickly.
• Light/Dark theme switcher and account password updates.
• AI Digest: summarize all saved highlights in one click.

★ PRIVACY & CLEAN UI
• Tooltips, modals, and the keywords tile use Shadow DOM so host-page CSS cannot break the UI.
• Email/password authentication keeps your highlights tied to your account.
• Copy, save, and manage your notes whenever you need them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. INSTALL & SIGN IN
   Add the extension, open the popup from the toolbar, and create an account or sign in.

2. CAPTURE TEXT
   Select any text on a webpage. Use Save, AI Summary, or Summarize Page from the tooltip.

3. EXPLORE KEYWORDS
   Use the Keywords tile to browse auto-detected terms. Click a word for a sectioned summary,
   then Copy or Save it to your dashboard.

4. REVIEW LATER
   Open the extension popup anytime to search, organize, theme, or generate an AI digest
   of everything you have saved.
